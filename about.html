Bio

A Different Kind of Law Student

Jay Haws is a JD/MBA candidate at Wake Forest University School of Law and School of Business, graduating May 2027. He is building toward a practice in corporate law or business litigation, with academic and practical experience that spans both.

His academic record reflects that direction. Jay has earned CALI honors, the highest grade in the class, in both Contracts and Corporate Governance. He serves as Problem Chair of Wake Forest's Transactional Law Team, was a finalist in both the Tulane and Villanova Professional Football Negotiation Competitions, and is an Online Editor for the Journal of Business and Intellectual Property Law. He also works as a Research Assistant to Professor Gregory Parks. His coursework spans Mergers and Acquisitions, Federal and Corporate Tax, and Contracts and Commercial Transactions Drafting.

Before law school, Jay earned a B.S. in Finance from the University of Minnesota, where he captained the Club Baseball team. Before that, he played Division III baseball and football, an experience that shaped his understanding of what it demands to compete in a team environment under real pressure.

That competitive background carried into entrepreneurship. Jay took over and managed the Stillwater Loggers, a collegiate summer baseball team in Stillwater, Minnesota, scheduling 30+ games annually, raising over $10K in funding across three years, and hosting free youth clinics in the community. He also worked in guest services with the Minnesota Twins, gaining exposure to how a Major League organization operates from the inside.

Professionally, Jay has drafted complaints, motions, and demand letters as a Legal Intern at Lester Law; supported client portfolio work at Highmark Wealth Management; and has served as a Compliance Legal Extern at Wake Forest Athletics since May 2026. Sports is a genuine part of his background, not just a specialty label.
